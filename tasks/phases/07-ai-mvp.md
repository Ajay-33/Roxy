# Phase 7: Evidence-based AI (MVP)

## Outcome

Roxy answers natural-language questions using exact queries and minimal retrieved memories, with evidence and uncertainty.

## Exit gate

- AI remains disabled when no provider key is configured.
- Exact questions use deterministic tools.
- Each factual claim has evidence or is labeled unsupported.
- Prompt-injection fixtures remain inert.
- Re-embedding is resumable and versioned.
- Seven-day soak meets reliability, privacy, backup, and battery success criteria.
