# Phase 8: Explainable Patterns

## Prerequisite

At least 2–4 weeks of sufficiently complete, corrected history.

## Outcome

Statistical detectors propose routines with sample size, evidence, confidence, freshness, and confirmation/dismissal controls.

## Exit gate

- Known synthetic histories produce expected patterns.
- Missing data lowers confidence.
- No causal, emotional, medical, financial, or relationship claim is presented as fact.
- Dismissed/corrected patterns stay respected.
