# Phase 9: Suggestions

## Outcome

Roxy offers rare, explainable, low-risk suggestions that the owner can silence immediately.

## Exit gate

- One-week shadow-mode review completed before notifications.
- Quiet hours, expiry, cooldown, daily cap, and feedback work.
- Initial live cap is one suggestion per day.
- Suggestions contain evidence and no autonomous action.
- Safety-critical, medical, financial, and relationship advice is excluded.
