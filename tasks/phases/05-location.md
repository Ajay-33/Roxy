# Phase 5: Location and Visits

## Outcome

Balanced mode creates reasonable visits with low battery impact; a visible time-bounded Trip mode offers optional detail.

## Exit gate

- Permission sequence and disable behavior work.
- Stationary, commute, short stop, poor GPS, reboot, and offline cases tested.
- Candidate place labels require owner confirmation.
- Raw sample compaction is enforced.
- Three comparable phone days show acceptable drain; a 20–25% breach blocks the phase.
