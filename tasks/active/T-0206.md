# T-0206: Three-day physical-phone accuracy and battery validation

- Status: in progress
- Phase: 2 / App usage
- Depends on: T-0205, T-0207

## Goal

Validate three consecutive real days of Roxy app-usage totals against Digital Wellbeing while measuring balanced-mode battery impact.

## Acceptance checks

- [x] Hosted prerequisite: T-0207 has deployed an HTTPS API, migrated managed PostgreSQL, and verified paired-device aggregate sync.
- [ ] Three local calendar days are collected and compared against Digital Wellbeing, with discrepancies documented.
- [ ] Reboot, offline, midnight, and timezone behavior are observed and documented.
- [ ] Battery impact is measured with a tuning decision against the 8% target and 12% warning threshold.
- [ ] No raw UsageEvents or app labels leave the phone; only aggregate-only behavior is inspected.
- [ ] Phase records, retention notes, and final checks are complete.

## Battery note

- Current collection is owner-triggered only; therefore this validation cannot establish balanced-mode background battery use until a separately scoped export/scheduling task exists.
- No foreground service, wake lock, or polling is enabled.

## Verification record

- Start date: 2026-08-20; Day 1 begins after the T-0208 simplified build was installed and phone controls were confirmed reachable.
- Day 1: not started.

- Blocking condition: none from hosting; the three real calendar days and physical battery measurement cannot be truthfully compressed into one session.

## Daily owner routine

1. Use the phone normally. Keep Usage Access enabled and leave the Roxy connection unchanged.
2. At roughly the same time each day, open Roxy and use **Read previous 24 hours**, **Create local 15-minute totals**, **Queue aggregate totals**, then **Sync now**.
3. Record Roxy's displayed total/count-only result beside Android Digital Wellbeing for the same local calendar day. Do not capture or share app names or raw event data.
4. On one day, leave the phone offline before **Sync now**, then reconnect and verify the pending upload later clears. On separate days, include one reboot, one midnight boundary, and one timezone check.
5. At the start and end of each day, record the phone battery percentage and Roxy's Android battery-usage entry. Compare it with normal use; the target is at most 8% extra over 24 hours, warning above 12%, reject at 20–25%.
