## Implementation notes

The total is a deterministic sum of `durationMillis` from `usage.bucket` aggregates. Ranking sorts by duration descending, then stored app identifier ascending. Both results remain incomplete until collector coverage is independently recorded; neither result describes missing periods as inactivity or sleep.
