# Non-negotiable Rules

This is the short version of the repository contract.

1. One task, one outcome, one reviewable change.
2. Collect only explicitly enabled signals from the owner's phone.
3. No hidden, invasive, or continuous audio/screen/accessibility monitoring.
4. Default balanced-mode battery target is at most 8% additional daily drain; 20–25% is the absolute rejection ceiling.
5. Offline-first: persist locally before sync, acknowledge before cleanup, and deduplicate retries.
6. Exact calculations use code/SQL; AI explains evidence and admits uncertainty.
7. Never expose real personal data or secrets in code, logs, tests, screenshots, or commits.
8. Every collector needs a toggle, health indicator, retention rule, deletion path, and battery test.
9. No new dependency, permission, or service without an explicit reason and task acceptance check.
10. Do not start the next phase until the current phase exit gate passes.
