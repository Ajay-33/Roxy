# Product Brief

## Vision

Roxy helps its owner remember and understand daily life using phone signals that are explicitly enabled, technically reliable, and proportionate to their battery and privacy cost.

## Product promise

Roxy should be:

- **Trustworthy:** facts are traceable to evidence and missing data is visible.
- **Private:** local-first collection, minimal upload, clear retention, export, and deletion.
- **Quiet:** no spam and no default behavior that materially harms battery life.
- **Correctable:** inferred places, patterns, and summaries can be fixed or dismissed.
- **Useful before intelligent:** timeline and exact analytics work before AI is added.

## Primary user journeys

### Understand today

The owner opens Today and sees collector health, places visited, app usage, selected notification counts, and a concise evidence-backed summary.

### Ask about the past

The owner asks a date-bounded question. Roxy computes exact values with queries, retrieves related memories, answers with evidence, and marks incomplete periods.

### Review an inference

Roxy proposes a place or routine. The owner sees why, confirms/corrects/dismisses it, and that correction affects later results.

### Control collection

The owner pauses everything or toggles a collector, sees exactly what it captures, checks last activity and battery impact, and deletes a date range.

## MVP capabilities

- Durable offline event queue and idempotent sync
- App-usage aggregation
- Filtered/redacted notification metadata and selected text
- Balanced location and derived visits
- Timeline and deterministic daily facts
- Daily review and corrections
- Evidence-based memory search and chat
- Diagnostics, pause, export, retention, and deletion

## Not MVP

- Autonomous actions
- Continuous audio, screen, accessibility, or network capture
- Financial or health conclusions
- Calendar, Health Connect, email, browser, or photo connectors
- Pattern-driven notifications
- Multiple users or Play Store distribution

## Success measures

For seven consecutive days:

- no unexplained multi-hour collection gaps;
- no event loss or duplication after offline sync;
- default balanced mode adds no more than 8% battery drain after tuning;
- deterministic regression questions return exact results;
- summaries cite evidence and admit incomplete data;
- export, range deletion, pause, and backup restore work.

## Product language

Use “observed,” “likely,” and “incomplete” honestly. Avoid “Roxy knows,” clinical claims, causal claims, judgmental labels, or certainty unsupported by evidence.
